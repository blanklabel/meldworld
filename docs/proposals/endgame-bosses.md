                                   ▼
                            ┌─────────────┐

                            │ cause of the │      behind every Shift. The season's climax.

**Defeat all three known bosses → the path to Ometus opens.** The hidden bosses are
**secret, discovery-gated, and optional** — they reward the personas who don't live on
the combat ladder. The whole ladder is **seasonal** (§"Seasons").

## The three known end-world bosses (the seasonal ladder)

Each is a **massive co-op raid** (the largest merges the battle engine allows, §"Scale")
with **unique mechanics** — the apex of `FS-4`'s "unique boss mechanics (not just big
stats)" remaining work — a guaranteed **rare-loot** payoff, and a **biome home** drawn
from the existing bestiary.

### Termina — the Devil in the Machine
- **Theme:** a devil fused to a dead god-engine; train / evil-machine, clockwork, rails,
  industry.
- **Biome home:** **The Seized Engine (Brass Corpse)** — the surface of a dead clockwork
  plane ([lore](../lore/shifting-lands.md)); Bludgeoning & Lightning; Tension Snaps,
  Arc-Bleed, casting-punishing Clockwork Phantoms. A perfect, already-written arena.
- **Signature mechanics (design seeds):** *phase rails* — a periodic "train" sweep across
  the arena you must be off; *reassembly* — destroyed mechanical adds **rebuild** unless
  their power source is cut; *overcharge* — punishes casters (the Phantom motif), rewarding
  martial/mixed comps; a mechanical multi-phase "restart."
- **Loot motif:** lightning/machine gear, clockwork cosmetics, a rare crafting material
  (feeds MS-1 / maker's-mark gear).

### Nestiph — the Goddess of Unlife & Rebirth
- **Theme:** myconid zombies, spore mind-control, rebirth as a violent industrial process.
- **Biome home:** **The Chitin-Kilns — the "Nestiphian Cradle"** ([lore](../lore/shifting-lands.md)) —
  *already named for her*: fermentation vats dissolving corpses to genetic slurry, the
  Rebirth Tax, the Newly-Hatched. Canonical home, ready-made.
- **Signature mechanics (design seeds):** *reanimation* — slain adds **come back** unless
  the vats/spawn are destroyed; *spore mind-control* — a hero (yours or an ally's) is
  **turned against the party** for a few turns (a corrupted-combatant mechanic on the ATB);
  *rebirth* — Nestiph **revives** at low HP unless a condition is met (burn the cradle
  first). Punishes slow, attrition-y fights — a DPS/priority check.
- **Loot motif:** necrotic/spore gear, a revive-themed consumable line, a rare reagent for
  Alchemy.

### Slake — the Demon Lord of Desire & Indulgence
- **Theme:** temptation, comfort-as-trap, gluttony, indulgence.
- **Biome home:** **Hearth-Plains (Velvet Steppe)** + the **Lotus-Engine (Apathy Resort)**
  — the desire/comfort-trap biomes ([lore](../lore/shifting-lands.md)): the Lover's Grasp,
  the Euphoric Siphon, rest-that-costs. *(These are currently attributed in lore to Ometus;
  see the reconciliation below — Slake inherits the desire domain.)*
- **Signature mechanics (design seeds):** *temptation* — lures of loot/comfort mid-fight
  that **punish greed** (grab the treasure → a debuff / it fights back); *the Charisma
  save* — a "rest and be safe" pull you must *reject* (a will check, not a damage check);
  *gluttony* — Slake **heals by consuming** (adds, dropped loot, the environment), so you
  starve it; *max-HP drain* (the Siphon motif). A discipline/greed check.
- **Loot motif:** decadent/opulent gear, indulgence cosmetics, the richest raw chit drop
  (the "reward for resisting reward").

## The True End Boss — Ometus

> **Ometus — the old, forgotten evil that spans space and time; the true cause of every
> Shift.** Unlocked only after all three known bosses fall. Ometus is *why the world
> rearranges* — the Shift (D20/§W2), which the game treats as a deterministic scheduler,
> is Ometus's influence bleeding through reality. Defeating it is the **season's ultimate
> achievement** and the meta-narrative climax.

- **Unlock:** the three-known-boss gate (scope — per world, per season — §Open).
- **Scale:** the single biggest encounter — the maximum raid the engine allows (§"Scale"),
  a multi-phase fight that should feel like the whole world showing up.
- **The Shift tie-in (the payoff):** because Ometus *causes* the Shifts, beating it has a
  **world consequence** — e.g. the Shifts **quiet** for the remainder of the season in that
  world (a hard-won stability the Builders have been fighting the Shift for all along), or
  a final cataclysmic Shift as it dies. Either way it closes the §W loop: "you spent the
  season pinning ground against the Shift; now you kill the thing behind it." (Exact
  consequence — §Open.)
- **Loot:** the apex — season-defining prestige cosmetics, a title, the top Vanguard glory,
  and the rarest gear/materials in the game.

## The hidden bosses — a secret apex for the non-combat personas

Off the critical path, **discovery-gated**, and deliberately aligned to the players who
*don't* live on the combat ladder ([core-loop-and-personas.md](core-loop-and-personas.md)).
Not required to reach Ometus (or, optionally, each grants an *edge* — §Open).

### All-Father — the mountain that is all slimes
- **Fiction:** a mountain-sized slime; every little slime in the world is **born from it**
  and, having "experienced the world," **returns to it** — a living cycle. The origin and
  drain of slime-kind.
- **Persona:** the **Gatherer / ecology** apex — found not by fighting deep but by
  *reading the living world* (following the slimes' migration/return, an ecology
  pilgrimage — ties [living-ecology.md](living-ecology.md) migration §D.3 + herds/swarms).
- **Mechanics (design seeds):** *fission* — sheds a **swarm** of little slimes (the CR
  swarm-battle mechanic at apex scale); *reabsorption* — pulls slimes back to **heal**, so
  you must clear or cut off the returners; the returning slimes carry what they "learned"
  (buffs from the world) — kill them before they get home.
- **Reward:** ecology/gatherer endgame — rarest materials, a **slime companion/mount**,
  collectables (CR-5) completion, a maker's-mark reagent.

### Terim — the God of Crafting & Building
- **Fiction:** the god who makes things; patron of every hand that shapes the world.
  Terim keeps the craft/build domain: the Foundry's state cult of **"One", the Forge
  God** is a **heresy of Terim** — a god of *making* industrialised into a god of
  *throughput* — not a rival deity, so this boss is unaffected. Terim's own followers
  hold the stabilisers and the Healery
  ([`lore/city-institutions.md`](../lore/city-institutions.md),
  [`lore/factions.md`](../lore/factions.md) → The Foundry).
- **Persona:** the **Builder / Crafter** apex — reached through **mastery of the craft,
  not combat depth**: a threshold of Forging/Alchemy/Mercantile level and/or structures
  built/anchors held (BD). You *earn the audience* by being a great maker.
- **Mechanics (design seeds):** a fight where **building & terrain matter** — you may
  *build* during it (raise walls/towers, Part of BD), out-*engineer* it as much as
  out-damage it; Terim tests your craft (wielding *your own* maker's-marked creations
  turned against you?). A boss designed so a Builder/Crafter comp shines.
- **Reward:** the crafter/builder endgame — **legendary recipes**, unique **blueprints**,
  **maker's-mark** unlocks (the reputation currency from [core-loop-and-personas.md](core-loop-and-personas.md)),
  a signature workshop structure.

## Reaching the bosses (ties the whole loop)

The bosses are the demand that *justifies* every persona's grind:
- **Adventurers** push to them via the deep world; the raid needs the best **gear**
  (crafters), a **supply road** of anchors/forward towns/portals to get there (builders,
  BD §W4), and **consumables/mats** (gatherers, merchants).
- **Builders** build the road to the door **and** chase **Terim**.
- **Gatherers/Crafters** stock the raid **and** chase **All-Father / Terim**.
- **Merchants** ride the **season's biggest demand spike** (raid consumables + gear).
So the boss ladder isn't just the Adventurer's ending — it's the **event that makes the
whole economy spike and cohere.**

## Scale, the battle engine & sim budget

- **Raid scale.** End-world bosses are the largest **battle merges** — D5 caps merges at
  2 instances (8) standard / 4 instances (16) for Gatekeepers; end-world bosses set the
  **top cap** (`endworld_raid_max_instances` **[TUNABLE]**), bounded by the **realm
  population cap** (`SC-3`) exactly like the mega-siege ([server-scaling.md](server-scaling.md)).
- **Unique mechanics = `FS-4` apex.** These bosses are precisely the "special attack
  patterns, not just big stats" `FS-4` flags as remaining — built on the existing
  `EncounterClass`/boss pipeline, party-scaled HP over a full merge, a proper arena.
- **No new sim class.** They're big `Battle`s (server-authoritative ATB) + a big
  arena; the overworld approach rides the existing world/siege budget (`CR-4`).

## Persistence & seasons

- **Boss-kill state is world + season scoped**, event-logged (§W5): which known bosses a
  world has felled this season, whether Ometus is unlocked/defeated. Replaying seed + log
  reconstructs it.
- **Seasonal (D8, 13 weeks).** The ladder is a **season-long race**: each season the
  known bosses reset and worlds race to the three → Ometus. First-to-Ometus is the
  season's headline. The Vanguard board ([endgame-seasons.md](../behaviors/endgame-seasons.md))
  gains **boss-kill lines** (first/fastest clears) beside max-distance.
- **Rewards persist across seasons** (titles, cosmetics, prestige, learned recipes/
  blueprints) per the §W5 / endgame-seasons "not wiped" rule; the *ladder progress*
  resets.
